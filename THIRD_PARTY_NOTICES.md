Nuclear Option and its game resources belong to their respective owners.
BepInEx, Harmony, Blueprinter and HeavyHangar remain separate dependencies under
their respective terms. Optional addon cargo requires the corresponding addon;
its models and plugin binaries are not included in this source snapshot.

This snapshot does not assign a new license to game resources, third-party
materials or the original project. No project-wide open-source license has been
selected by the author in this preparation step.
